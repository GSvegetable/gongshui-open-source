# Asset and data licensing

The MIT license in this directory covers original project source code and authored styles. It does not automatically relicense:

- Contributor names, profiles, avatars, GitHub identities, or submitted notes in `data/contributors.json`.
- Third-party Earth imagery, topology maps, flag artwork, or geographic datasets in `assets/` and `data/`.
- Third-party libraries such as Globe.GL / Three.js and their transitive dependencies.

Before redistributing those materials, identify their original sources and comply with their licenses and attribution requirements. You may integrate the globe source code under MIT while supplying your own properly licensed Earth textures, flag assets, and contributor dataset.
